Once the NHL playoffs begin, Oilers Score shows the full bracket — every series, round by round, for both conferences.

## Before you start

- The **🏆 Playoff Picture** button only appears during the playoffs. Outside the postseason there's nothing to show.

## Steps

1. Tap the **🏆 Playoff Picture** button at the top of the screen.
2. In the sheet, scroll through the **Eastern Conference** and **Western Conference** brackets.
3. Read each series for the matchup, the series score, and who leads or has won; later rounds appear as teams advance.
4. Scroll to the bottom for the **Stanley Cup Final**.
5. Tap the **✕** to close the bracket.

## What you'll see

The full bracket for the current playoffs. The team you follow is highlighted in its accent color so you can spot its path through the rounds.
