The Player Stats view breaks a game down to each player — goals, assists, ice time, and goalie numbers — for both teams.

## Steps

1. Open a game's **Game Details** view and make sure the **Player Stats** tab is selected.
2. Use the two **team buttons** at the top to switch between the away and home team.
3. Read the **skaters** table: goals, assists, points, plus/minus, penalty minutes, shots on goal, and time on ice.
4. Scroll to the **goalies** table for shots against, goals against, saves, and save percentage. A ★ marks the starter.

## What you'll see

A full box score for the selected team, with a totals row at the bottom. Tapping the other team button re-renders the table for that side.
