When the Oilers are playing, the top card turns into a live scoreboard that updates on its own. This guide explains what each part of it means.

## Steps

1. Open the app during a game. The top card shows a red **Live** badge.
2. Read the **score** for each team in the score row.
3. Check the **period and clock** below the score for the period (1st, 2nd, 3rd, OT, SO) and time remaining.
4. Look at the **shots on goal** row to compare each team's shot totals.
5. Watch for a **power play** badge when a team has the man advantage.
6. Scroll the card for the **Period Scores** line score, goalie details, the venue, and TV broadcasts.

## What you'll see

A live scoreboard that refreshes about every 30 seconds, so the score, clock, and shots stay current without a manual refresh. When the game ends, the badge changes to **Final** and goalie decisions (Win / Loss) appear.
