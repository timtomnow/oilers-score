The Play-by-Play tab lists every play in a game. Filters let you jump straight to goals, penalties, or a single period instead of scrolling the whole feed.

## Steps

1. Open a game's **Game Details** view and tap the **Play-by-Play** tab.
2. Tap a type pill to filter the feed: **All**, **Goals**, **Penalties**, or **Shots**.
3. Under **Per**, tap one or more period pills to limit the feed to those periods.
4. Tap an active pill again to turn it off and widen the feed.

## What you'll see

The play list updates immediately to match your filters — for example, **Goals** in the **3rd** shows only third-period goals. Goals and penalties are emphasized, and each play is tagged with the team that made it.
